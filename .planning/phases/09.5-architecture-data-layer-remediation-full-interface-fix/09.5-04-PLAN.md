<objective>
Turn tRPC and Hono into thin adapters over `src/application/**`, create the missing domain routers, and remove the duplicate product-kernel Hono API.
</objective>

- T-04-02: Removing product-kernel router breaks OpenAPI routes. Mitigation: Hono adapter tests assert `/api/v1/openapi.json` and migrated route behavior before deletion.
- Block on: high.
</threat_model>
